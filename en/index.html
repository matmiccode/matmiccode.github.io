Shared principles

Every MATCODE app works the same way, whatever it does.

Your files stay with you
Results and settings live on your disk. The app connects to the internet only for what it needs to do its job, and its page lists what and when.
No account, no ads
Install and use. There is no registration, no login, no telemetry and no ads.
Open source
The code of every app is public under the MIT license. You can check what it does, report a bug or build it yourself.
Signed releases
The app checks for a new version by itself and installs only releases signed with the author's key. It installs in your user folder, without administrator rights.

Support

The apps are free and will stay free. If one of them saved you an hour, you can buy the author a coffee.
